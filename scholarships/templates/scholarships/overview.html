Program Overview

What this scholarship recognizes

The program exists to support graduating 18U players who exemplify excellence both on and off the field. It reflects VCB’s commitment to developing strong athletes, accountable leaders, engaged students, and positive community members.

  • Values-driven selection focused on overall impact, character, and growth
  • Support for post-secondary, trade, vocational, and recognized development pathways
  • Annual recognition of players who embody the values of Vancouver Community Baseball

Application Checklist

What applicants should prepare

  • Applicant information and VCB participation details
  • Post-graduation plans and intended field of study or training
  • A 300–500 word statement about growth, leadership, commitment, and community impact
  • One required reference and one optional second reference from non-family adults
  • Declarations, consent, and optional supporting documents
